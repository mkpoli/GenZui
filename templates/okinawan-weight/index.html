Method, limits and source records

Measurements use a fixed 1,000-unit em. Filled outlines are united before area and perimeter are measured. Perimeter uses the Bézier outlines, rather than a raster boundary. Native glyphs are read at Noto’s 400 and 700 instances. Native script ranges include encoded glyphs only; aliases are deduplicated within each script.

Stroke diameters use a binary raster at 1,024 pixels per em and a Euclidean distance transform on its medial axis. A 512-pixel run checks sampling sensitivity. Axis samples are weighted by local length. Neighbourhoods of branch points and terminals are excluded; nearby thin strokes can also be excluded. Direction bins below 60 units of supported axis length are hidden. Direction estimates that differ by more than 10% across resolutions are omitted. Whole-character medians mix stems, hairlines, joins and marks.

Greater ink coverage can come from more stroke length without thicker strokes. A kana with an added vowel or mark therefore need not match the ink area of its donor. The numbers locate differences; the same-size specimens are needed to judge optical balance.
