Japanese text and historical kana

Unified serif · Regular · version 0.002 · Unicode 18

One font contains Noto Serif JP’s full 16,726-character repertoire and 307 additional historical kana: 17,033 encoded characters in total. All 286 hentaigana and all seven Unicode 18 kana additions are included.

Seventeen forms are provisional. Their joins, proportions and stroke weight need typographic review. “HK Serif Proof” is a temporary font-menu identifier. Regular is the only available weight.

Coverage and licence

The historical inventory contains 309 characters: 286 hentaigana, 11 other letters and digraphs in Kana Supplement and Kana Extended-A, ten small kana, and ゟ and ヿ. The base uses Japanese regional glyph forms. This build does not include the whole Chinese and Korean repertoires or the CJK-encoded kana ligatures U+2A708, U+2CEFF, U+2CF00 and U+2CF02.

The font derives from Noto Serif JP and Noto Serif Hentaigana and is licensed under SIL OFL 1.1. New forms use Noto components and original drawing. No outline data was extracted from Unicode charts.
