源萃げんずい明朝 · GenZui Serif

Japanese text and historical kana · Regular · {{VERSION}} development build · Unicode 18

One font contains Noto Serif JP’s full 16,726-character repertoire and 338 additional historical kana and transcription characters: 17,064 encoded characters in total. All 286 hentaigana, all seven Unicode 18 kana additions, and the four kana ligatures encoded in CJK blocks are included.

Twenty-one constructed forms are provisional. Their joins, proportions and stroke weight need typographic review. Regular is the only available weight. The installed family appears as “GenZui Serif” or “源萃明朝”, depending on the application’s language.

Coverage and licence

The historical inventory contains 340 characters: 286 hentaigana, 11 other letters and digraphs in Kana Supplement and Kana Extended-A, ten small kana, ゟ and ヿ, four CJK-encoded kana ligatures, 13 Minnan tone letters, two support marks, SQUARE PAATU (㌬), 卄, five ideographic tally marks and five ideographic-description symbols. Shared ideographs use Japanese regional forms. 卄 is added from Noto Serif CJK JP; other Chinese and Korean coverage follows the Noto Serif JP base.

GenZui Serif combines Noto Serif JP, Noto Serif Hentaigana, Noto Serif CJK JP and FRB Taiwanese Kana (SIL OFL 1.1). Constructed forms use Noto kana components and original drawing. The combined font is distributed under SIL OFL 1.1. Source credits · Jigmo CC0 dedication
