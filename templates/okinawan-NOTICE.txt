GenZui Serif Okinawan / 源萃明朝 沖縄文字

A repertoire subset of GenZui Serif, distributed under SIL OFL 1.1.
The retained outlines, metrics and shaping rules match the full parent family.

Noto Serif JP supplies the ordinary kana, Latin letters, numbers, punctuation,
combining dakuten and components of the Okinawan constructions. The Google,
Adobe and Noto Project notices are retained in OFL.txt and the font metadata.
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notoserifjp

GenZui supplies 18 Funatsu bases and eight raised katakana from Noto components.
Voiced forms and YI/YE use combining dakuten. Private-use mappings follow
Nishiki-teki and Xim Sans conventions; see okinawan-mappings.json.
The character-system references identify the letters; the outlines are GenZui
constructions from Noto components.

FRB Taiwanese Kana by Fredrick R. Brennan supplies U+0305 and U+0323.
GenZui provides their kana attachment and contextual vertical placement.
https://github.com/ctrlcctrlv/FRBTaiwaneseKana
See FRB-OFL.txt and FRB-README.md.

sources.json identifies the parent font hashes. source-manifest.json records
the full family's pinned sources. Unicode data terms are in Unicode-LICENSE.txt.
