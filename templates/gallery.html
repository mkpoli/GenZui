References

The connected KOTO and the balance of TOKI were checked against the Unicode chart and printed examples reproduced in L2/24-150: 手島継蔵『国語漢文自習要覧』(1916, figure 2), 『普通日本文典』(1893, figure 9) and 松川半山『童蒙画引小学入門』(1875, figure 16). Hiragana NARI follows the Japanese JMJ-056850 reference in the Extension F chart.

Native Noto kana guide the stroke weight and terminals. The historical specimens inform structure; their printed outlines are not included in the font. NARI references include printed forms in 糸左近『和漢の文典：雅俗対照』(1902), p. 60, and 藤井乙男『操觚便覧』(1899), p. 16. Open each NARI card’s references to see the original scans. SHITE uses an enlarged Noto SHI dot. TOMO retains MO’s native shoulder and lower return.

Using the WU alternate

In applications with OpenType controls, enable stylistic set 1, “Hooked WU”. In CSS:

font-family: "GenZui Serif", serif;
font-feature-settings: "ss01" 1;

Leave ss01 off for the curved form. Both use U+1B11F, so copying and searching preserve the same text.
