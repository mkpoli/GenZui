    04 / LAYOUT組版機能

    Layout features.

    OpenType features found in each font’s GSUB and GPOS tables, named after the OpenType feature registry.

    {{FEATURES}}

    Vertical text

    Each column is the line 、。「」ーゃっ shaped top to bottom by HarfBuzz, the engine of Chromium and Firefox, and drawn from the font’s own outlines. The cells are one em tall.

    {{VERTICAL}}

    The GenZui fonts replace all seven characters with vertical forms through vert and vrt2. Sukima Gothic has no GSUB or GPOS table. In Chromium, its Apple morx table places 、。「」 vertically; ー stays horizontal and the small kana ゃ and っ keep their ordinary glyphs. A text engine that ignores morx would show none of these substitutions; this was not tested. Sukima’s readme warns that punctuation positions in vertical text may be off.

    08 / METHOD測定方法

    Method and sources.

    Generated by scripts/compare_fonts.py into research/font-comparison.json.

    Code points are the cmap of each file, counted against the assigned characters of Blocks.txt and UnicodeData.txt for Unicode 18.0. The {{HENTAIGANA_COUNT}} hentaigana are U+1B001–U+1B11E. The {{UNICODE18_COUNT}} Unicode 18 kana are the characters of the kana blocks that DerivedAge.txt dates to 18.0.

    Overlap is the ink shared by two 200 px renders on a common baseline divided by their combined ink. Shape correlation is described under Hentaigana. Both are likeness measures and do not prove where a drawing came from; the provenance section rests on the quoted documents. Vertical columns are HarfBuzz output drawn from the outlines.
