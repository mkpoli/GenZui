GenZui Serif / 源萃明朝 (げんずい)

Noto Serif JP: full Japanese base and historical-kana construction components.
GenZui: twenty-one constructed forms using Noto kana components and original drawing.
WU has a curved default and a hooked stylistic alternate (ss01).
Noto Serif Hentaigana: 290 historical forms, combining marks and small YE components.
The Google, Adobe and Noto Project notices are retained in OFL.txt.
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notoserifjp
https://github.com/notofonts/hentaigana

FRB Taiwanese Kana by Fredrick R. Brennan, SIL OFL 1.1:
13 Minnan tone letters and U+0305/U+0323 combining marks.
GenZui supplies kana attachment and contextual vertical tone placement.
https://github.com/ctrlcctrlv/FRBTaiwaneseKana
See sources/upstream/FRBTaiwaneseKana/OFL.txt and README.md.

Jigmo2, 2025-09-12, by Koichi Kamichi and GlyphWiki contributors:
Historical comparison fonts through 0.102 use its U+2CF02 outline.
The current font replaces that outline with a GenZui drawing.
The source outline is dedicated under CC0 1.0.
See sources/upstream/Jigmo/LICENSE.txt, README.txt and THANKS.txt.
https://kamichikoichi.github.io/jigmo/
