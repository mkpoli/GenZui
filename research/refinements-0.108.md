# GenZui Serif 0.108

Four forms have revised outlines. Curved WU remains the default, with Hooked WU
available through `ss01`.

| Form | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Hooked WU | The stem descends almost vertically and eases left toward the hook. The outward bow and reversal of direction are removed. |
| Katakana NARI | The lower entry and neck retain their weight. The bowl is lighter, the shoulder moves 16 units inward, and the right end moves inward to balance the sweep. |
| Alternate NE | The middle crossbar rises 50 units; its weight and leftward extension are retained. |
| Alternate WI | Each vertical stroke moves 36 units inward. The strokes retain their outlines and weight, with the horizontal bars unchanged. |

Katakana NARI’s lower stroke has about 16% less ink than 0.107. Its upper
stroke is unchanged. The stem of Hooked WU retains approximately 62 units of
width and moves consistently leftward through its descent.

TOMO, hiragana NARI and SHITE retain their 0.107 outlines. KOTO E, TOKI and TOTE,
YORI and curved WU are unchanged. The gallery compares the four revised forms
with 0.107 in horizontal and vertical writing; unchanged forms appear once.
