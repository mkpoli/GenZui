# GenZui Serif 0.111

Hooked WU has a shorter return set farther right. Its tip moves 95 units right,
and the bottom of the bend moves 46 units right. The upper bow and crossbars
keep their previous outlines.

The lower stem is redrawn as a continuous curve into the heel. Its inner edge
follows a 62-unit inward offset, with matching tangents at the upper join.
The return then tapers toward its tip. Native Noto か and カ remain in the
reference panel for comparison of kana turns and tapers.

Curved WU remains the default; `ss01` selects Hooked WU. All encoded glyphs
retain their 0.110 outlines. The gallery compares the hooked alternate against
0.110 in both horizontal and vertical writing, with synchronized swapping.
