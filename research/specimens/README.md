# NARI printed references

National Diet Library IIIF crops, retrieved 2026-09-21. Both source manifests
identify access as PDM (public domain). These scans guide the skeleton and brush
entry; GenZui outlines are drawn separately.

- `nari-katakana-1899.jpg`: 藤井乙男編『操觚便覧』浜本明昇堂, 1899, p. 16.
  [Page 12](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/863176/1/12), region 1870,600,300,440.
- `nari-hiragana-1902.jpg`: 糸左近『和漢の文典：雅俗対照』金刺芳流堂, 1902, p. 60.
  [Page 36](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/864212/1/36), region 3150,1150,300,1150.

The 1899 form has a shallow lower sweep. The 1902 hiragana form has a swept
upper entry and low wave. Proportions differ from the Japanese JMJ-056850
reference in the Unicode chart; GenZui retains the chart’s longer diagonal.
