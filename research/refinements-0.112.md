# TOKI and TOTE proportions, 0.112

TOTE (𛄥, U+1B125) uses a uniform 92% optical size; TOKI (𛄤, U+1B124)
uses 90%. Both reductions share TOMO's optical centre at (500, 365) in the
1000-unit em. The components and their connections are scaled together.

| Form | 0.111 outline size | 0.112 outline size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TOMO 𪜈 | 770 × 734 | 770 × 734 |
| TOTE 𛄥 | 839 × 812 | 772 × 746 |
| TOKI 𛄤 | 839 × 800 | 755 × 720 |

TOTE's width and upper edge align closely with TOMO. TOKI occupies a slightly
smaller area. Advances remain one em in horizontal and vertical text, and
vertical origins stay fixed. Both WU variants and every other glyph retain
their 0.111 outlines and metrics.

The gallery compares the 0.111 and 0.112 forms at the same em and baseline.
The sequence 𪜈𛄥𛄤 appears in both writing directions. Each revised card has
a swap control that changes its outline and reading samples together.
