# GenZui Serif 0.109

Hooked WU has one broad outward arc from the upper bar to the short hook.
Each side of the main stem is a single quadratic curve. Curvature is distributed
through the full descent, with its tangent continuing into the lower return.
Curved WU remains the default; `ss01` selects Hooked WU.

NE’s middle crossbar is halfway between its 0.107 and 0.108 positions: 25 units
lower than 0.108. WI’s two stems each move 18 units outward from 0.108, placing
them halfway between the previous two spacings. Their stroke weights and the
horizontal bars are retained.

Katakana NARI retains its accepted 0.108 outline and appears once in the gallery.
All other forms are unchanged. The three revised forms have 0.108 comparisons,
with swaps in horizontal and vertical writing.
