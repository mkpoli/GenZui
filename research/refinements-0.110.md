# GenZui Serif 0.110

Only Hooked WU changes. Its upper bow and crossbars retain their 0.109 shape.
The lower return uses the heel and leftward tip of Noto Serif JP か at native
scale, translated 160 units right and 48 units down. New connecting curves
match the tangents of the descending bow and the native heel.

In the source か, the outer descent rounds into a broad heel. The inner edge
opens into the lighter leftward return. Noto カ provides a second comparison,
with a more compact turn. These two native kana appear in WU’s reference panel.
The revised WU uses the rounder hiragana construction.

The outline comparisons use the pinned Noto source, which supplies the direct
geometric reference. [Adobe’s account of Source Han Serif’s design](https://blog.adobe.com/jp/publish/2018/04/10/dtp-font-adobetype-30th-2)
describes the study of brush-written kana and the restrained use of those forms
in the finished typeface.

NE and WI retain their 0.109 outlines. WI is accepted and appears once, as do
all other unchanged forms. Curved WU remains the default; `ss01` selects Hooked
WU. Its 0.109 comparison includes horizontal and vertical reading samples.
