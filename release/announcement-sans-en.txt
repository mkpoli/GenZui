【✍️ GenZui Sans 源萃ゴシック 💫】

Built on Noto Sans JP and Noto Sans Hentaigana, with

・all 286 hentaigana
・every small kana
・the Unicode 18.0 kana additions
・every encoded kana ligature
・the full Ainu katakana set
・Minnan (Taiwanese) kana tone letters

A free, open font (SIL OFL 1.1) 🚀 The same historical repertoire as GenZui Serif, in a gothic face.
