GenZui Sans 0.104 supports Okinawan kana in Regular and Bold. The 27 Funatsu forms include ligatures such as とぅ・てぃ・くゎ and letters that distinguish word-initial sounds. Eight raised katakana support the Okinawa prefectural notation.

Choose a package:

- **GenZuiSans-0.104.zip** — full family, 17,096 encoded characters, including kanji and historical kana.
- **GenZuiSansOkinawan-0.104.zip** — sentence-ready subset, 805 encoded characters: kana, punctuation, Latin, combining marks and the Okinawan forms.

Both include TTF and WOFF2 files, an editable specimen, CSS and licenses. Private-use characters need a compatible font; the mapping file is included.

The release preserves every glyph and metric from Sans 0.103. Published Serif fonts are unchanged. Distributed under SIL OFL 1.1.
